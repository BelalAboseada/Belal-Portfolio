The mark

Monogram

The mark is a single ribbon folded into a B. The top arm turns down into the upper bowl, creases back along a 36.3° diagonal, and opens into the lower bowl — one continuous path, no overlaps. It is the only logo element that may appear alone, and it is never redrawn: use the supplied vector.

(02)

Logo versions

One flat colour, always

The mark carries no gradient, no second tone and no outline. It is set in one step of the Flax Smoke ramp — or in pure black or white where the reproduction is one-colour. Contrast against the surface is the only rule that decides which.

Clear space

X = stem width

Keep a margin of X — the stem width of the mark — clear on all four sides. Nothing enters that field: no type, no rules, no photography, no edge of the page.

Minimum clear space on all sides · X = 0.205 H
(06)

Minimum size

24 px / 8 mm

Drag to see the mark at true size. Below 24 px wide the aperture between the top bar and the stem closes and the fold stops reading. In print the floor is 8 mm. The full lockup needs 88 px; below that, use the mark alone.

Colour

The identity has no hue of its own beyond the ramp already in global.css. Flax Smoke carries every surface and every piece of text; step 500 is the accent, because that is what the site already uses for selection. Black and white are reserved for one-colour reproduction. Typography

Bricolage Grotesque

One typeface carries the brand. global.css forces Bricolage Grotesque onto every heading, paragraph and span, so it is the voice by default. Cabinet Grotesk is the title face and appears only where .font-title is applied.
